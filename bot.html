🤖 VilvBot Documentation

VilvBot is an automated web crawler designed for search engine indexing. We aim to be a "good citizen" of the web by following industry-standard crawling etiquette.

Verified Bot Status: This bot is configured to meet Cloudflare's verification requirements, including unique identification and cryptographic request signing.

Identification

Our crawler identifies itself using the following User-Agent string:

VilvBot/1.0 (+https://user447-ui.github.io/bot.html)

Crawling Policy

  • Robots.txt: We strictly honor all Disallow directives.
  • Crawl-Delay: We respect the crawl-delay setting to prevent server overload (mandatory for Cloudflare-protected zones).
  • Frequency: Our distributed architecture ensures we do not perform excessive scraping on any single host.

Verification & Security

To prevent spoofing and ensure transparency, VilvBot signs its requests using Ed25519 cryptography. This allows site owners and infrastructure providers to verify that the traffic originated from our official crawler.

How to Opt-Out

If you wish to prevent VilvBot from indexing your site, please add the following rules to your robots.txt file:

User-agent: VilvBot
Disallow: /

Purpose of Service

The collected data is used exclusively for building a search index. We do not perform scalping, credential stuffing, or any malicious scanning activities. Our operations are benign and intended for the benefit of the research community.
